# Critique

Adapted and trimmed from pbakaus/impeccable reference/critique.md (Apache-2.0, Copyright 2025
Paul Bakaus). Changed: shortened and rewritten for Casper.

Report only: change no files.

1. Open the page at 390 wide and at 1280 wide (`viewport`, then `screenshot` at each). `inspect` it
   for its text and controls. Try the main action once if it only touches local test data.
2. Score Nielsen's 10 heuristics 0 to 4 in a table: heuristic, score, the one finding behind it.
   1 Visibility of status · 2 Match with the real world · 3 User control and freedom ·
   4 Consistency and standards · 5 Error prevention · 6 Recognition over recall ·
   7 Flexibility and efficiency · 8 Aesthetic and minimal design · 9 Help with errors ·
   10 Help and documentation.
   4 means really good, not fine; most pages score 20 to 32 of 40. Write n/a, with a reason, for
   one that cannot apply (help on a landing page), and give the total out of what you scored.
3. Then write:
   - What works: 2 or 3 things, and why.
   - Issues: the 3 to 5 that matter most, each P0 to P3 (P0 stops the task, P1 makes it hard,
     P2 an annoyance with a way round, P3 polish): what, why it matters, the fix, and the /look
     command that would make it (/look polish, /look adapt, /look clarify ...).
   - Does it look made for this product, or like any product's page? Name machine-made signs you
     see: a small label above each heading, 01 / 02 / 03 on things that are not steps, gradient
     text, cards inside cards, purple gradients, emoji as icons, vague slogans.
   - Last, up to 3 questions for the user, only where an answer would change a fix or its order.
Keep it short, and say which screenshots you looked at.
