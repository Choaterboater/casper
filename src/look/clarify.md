# Clarify

Adapted and trimmed from pbakaus/impeccable reference/clarify.md (Apache-2.0, Copyright 2025
Paul Bakaus). Changed: shortened and rewritten for Casper.

Wording only: change the words on the page, not its layout, styles or behaviour. Keep facts and
product terms, and ask before changing a claim.

1. Open it at 1280 wide; `inspect` every heading, button, label, empty state and error you can reach.
2. Rewrite:
   - a button says what happens ("Save changes", not "Submit"), and one action keeps one name
     ("Publish", then "Published");
   - labels stay visible; a placeholder is only an example;
   - an error says what failed and how to put it right, with no blame and no codes;
   - an empty state says what will appear and how to add the first one;
   - loading says what is happening ("Saving…");
   - plain words over internal ones, each idea once, sentence case.
3. Record a `text` check for changed words you can reach, replay it after the edit, and screenshot
   again. List each change as before and after.
