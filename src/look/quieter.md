# Quieter

Adapted and trimmed from pbakaus/impeccable reference/quieter.md (Apache-2.0, Copyright 2025
Paul Bakaus). Changed: shortened and rewritten for Casper.

Theme tokens only: calm a page that is too loud through its colour, shadow, border, radius and
weight variables where the theme has them, never raw values in components. Keep its character:
quiet is not gray and generic.

1. Screenshot at 390 and 1280 wide. Name what makes it loud: saturated colours, many accents,
   heavy weights everywhere, strong shadows or glows, motion, everything big.
2. Decide the one or two things that stay bold; everything else recedes.
3. Change tokens: accents less saturated, one accent used rarely, softer shadows and thinner
   borders, weights a step down (700 to 600), less motion. Never gray text on a colour.
4. Keep the contrast: text 4.5:1 in light and dark, worked out from the values; show the numbers
   before and after.
5. Replay checks and screenshot both widths again.
